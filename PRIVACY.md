# Skills Shop Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This notice explains how Skills Shop handles your data. Available features depend on your app version and account access.

## On your Mac

Your local library, edits and settings stay on your Mac unless you use a backup feature. Browsing the Shop or checking for updates does not upload your local skills. Scanning supported tools reads their skill folders; you choose what to import.

Deleted library content may remain in local recovery copies. Uninstalling the app does not automatically remove your library or skills installed in other tools.

## Accounts and cloud backup

Google sign-in provides your name, email and profile picture. Supabase handles authentication, the Shop and private cloud storage. GitHub hosts public app downloads. These services receive connection information, such as your IP address, when you use them.

Cloud backup can store skill names, sources, versions, device information and complete skill files, including scripts and attachments. Unmodified Shop skills may be saved as references to their public versions. Private backups are restricted to your account and are not published to the Shop.

Older versions ask you to confirm file uploads. In versions with Pro automatic backup, eligible Pro accounts back up their library by default, respecting any previous pause or exclusions. You can pause backup or exclude skills. Pausing or signing out stops new uploads; a transfer already in progress may finish. Neither action deletes existing backups. Check skills for secrets before backing them up: automated checks cannot catch everything.

If you configure Git backup, your chosen Git provider also handles that data.

## Optional usage statistics

In versions that offer usage statistics, they are off by default. If you enable them, PostHog receives basic app actions, outcomes, timings, app and operating system versions, and a random installation identifier. These statistics are not linked to your signed-in account. They exclude skill contents, names, file paths, search terms, credentials and screen recordings.

Turning statistics off stops collection and removes the local identifier. It does not erase data already received by the service.

## Retention and deletion

While cloud access is active, the current version is retained. Older versions are kept for up to 30 days, with at most 10 per skill. After Pro access expires, you have 30 days to export existing backups. Access then ends and cloud content may be deleted; renewing access cannot recover content already deleted.

Download usage records are kept for 90 days; monthly summaries cover the current month and the previous three months. Service records used for access control and usage accounting do not contain skill contents.

Removing a local skill or uninstalling the app does not delete cloud backups. Deleting cloud data does not remove copies on your devices.

For account or cloud data deletion, email [sunzybd@gmail.com](mailto:sunzybd@gmail.com) from your account email and describe what you want removed. We verify your identity before processing requests. There is currently no self-service option to delete your account or all cloud history. Do not send passwords, tokens or private skill files.
